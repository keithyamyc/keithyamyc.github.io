**Thursday, 2 April**

Arrived at Songshan at 4:40, rain. The kind of rain that does not fall so much as hang. Took the MRT to Zhongshan because I could not remember the name of the hotel and did not want to admit it.

The room is small and has a window that opens onto an air-conditioning unit and, beyond it, a wall with a painted bird on it. I like it more than I should.

Walked to a bookshop on Chifeng Street at 8. Stayed until they turned the lights down. Bought two books I cannot read quickly and a notebook I do not need.

Dinner: beef noodles, 180, at a place with no English menu and four stools. The broth had star anise and something sweeter underneath. I ate too fast.

**Friday, 3 April**

Woke at 6 because of the bird-wall light. Went out for soy milk and youtiao. Sat on a low wall in a small park and watched three old men do something between tai chi and an argument.

Spent the afternoon at the palace museum. I had intended to look at the calligraphy and instead spent an hour in front of a single bronze. There is a particular green that bronze gets after two thousand years. I have no vocabulary for it.

> Rain all day. My shoes are finished. I do not care.

Evening: met C. for coffee. We have not seen each other since 2019. It was not awkward, which surprised me. We talked about our parents, mostly. She said something about her father that I have been thinking about since: that he became more himself as he got smaller.

**Saturday, 4 April**

Last day. Bought a film roll — Portra 400, 120 — from a shop that also sold fountain pens and model trains. The owner asked what camera. When I told him, he nodded once, as if confirming a suspicion.

Took eleven photographs. Probably three will be usable. The light was flat and I did not care.

Flight at 7:20. At the airport I realised I had written nothing about the thing I came to think about. That is usually how it goes. The thinking happens on the plane home, and by then the notebook is in the bag.

Read the whole way back. Landed in rain.
